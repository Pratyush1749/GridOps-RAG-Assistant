Monthly Reliability Report — Mountain West — June 2026

Monthly reliability report for the Mountain West region covering June 2026. This report summarizes distribution reliability performance for all feeders in the region and is prepared for internal review and regulatory submission.

During the reporting period the region recorded 2 outage events 2 at severity P4. 2 events were restored within the period, accounting for approximately 479,339 customer-minutes of interruption across 1,061 customer interruptions.

Calculated reliability indices for Mountain West in June 2026: SAIDI 9398.80 minutes per customer, SAIFI 20.804 interruptions per customer, CAIDI 451.8 minutes per interrupted customer. These figures are calculated against a served base of 51 metered customers in the region.

Methodology note: SAIDI is the sum of customers affected multiplied by outage duration in minutes, divided by total customers served. SAIFI is total customer interruptions divided by total customers served. CAIDI is SAIDI divided by SAIFI. Momentary interruptions cleared by automatic reclose are excluded from these figures and are tracked separately under MAIFI. Major event days, where daily SAIDI exceeds the statistical threshold, are excluded from routine reporting and reported separately.
