Monthly Reliability Report — Midwest — February 2026

Monthly reliability report for the Midwest region covering February 2026. This report summarizes distribution reliability performance for all feeders in the region and is prepared for internal review and regulatory submission.

During the reporting period the region recorded 6 outage events 1 at severity P2, 4 at severity P3, 1 at severity P4. 5 events were restored within the period, accounting for approximately 2,152,279 customer-minutes of interruption across 9,575 customer interruptions.

Calculated reliability indices for Midwest in February 2026: SAIDI 20497.90 minutes per customer, SAIFI 91.190 interruptions per customer, CAIDI 224.8 minutes per interrupted customer. These figures are calculated against a served base of 105 metered customers in the region.

Methodology note: SAIDI is the sum of customers affected multiplied by outage duration in minutes, divided by total customers served. SAIFI is total customer interruptions divided by total customers served. CAIDI is SAIDI divided by SAIFI. Momentary interruptions cleared by automatic reclose are excluded from these figures and are tracked separately under MAIFI. Major event days, where daily SAIDI exceeds the statistical threshold, are excluded from routine reporting and reported separately.
