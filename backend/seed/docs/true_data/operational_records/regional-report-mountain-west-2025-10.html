Monthly Reliability Report — Mountain West — October 2025

Monthly reliability report for the Mountain West region covering October 2025. This report summarizes distribution reliability performance for all feeders in the region and is prepared for internal review and regulatory submission.

During the reporting period the region recorded 2 outage events 1 at severity P3, 1 at severity P4. 2 events were restored within the period, accounting for approximately 986,212 customer-minutes of interruption across 2,958 customer interruptions.

Calculated reliability indices for Mountain West in October 2025: SAIDI 19337.49 minutes per customer, SAIFI 58.000 interruptions per customer, CAIDI 333.4 minutes per interrupted customer. These figures are calculated against a served base of 51 metered customers in the region.

Methodology note: SAIDI is the sum of customers affected multiplied by outage duration in minutes, divided by total customers served. SAIFI is total customer interruptions divided by total customers served. CAIDI is SAIDI divided by SAIFI. Momentary interruptions cleared by automatic reclose are excluded from these figures and are tracked separately under MAIFI. Major event days, where daily SAIDI exceeds the statistical threshold, are excluded from routine reporting and reported separately.
